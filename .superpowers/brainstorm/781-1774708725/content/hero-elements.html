What to add around the heading?

The background gets warm gradients + patterns. Now pick which elements to layer on top. Select all that feel right.

1

Tagline below heading

Add "Мир вкуса и уюта" in italic serif beneath the big title. Gives context and softens the boldness.

2

Decorative separator

A thin ornamental line + dot between heading and tagline (like the swirl separators used on other sections). Adds elegance.

3

Floating accent dots

Subtle animated dots drifting slowly in the background. Already used in other chapters of this page -- brings consistency.

4

Scroll indicator

A small animated arrow or "scroll" text at the bottom of the hero. Tells visitors there's more below the fold.
