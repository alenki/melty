Design Notes

Photo Mix

~18 photos from food (38 available), ~8 from inside (25 available), ~6 from items (17 available). Mixed organically, not grouped by category. 3 quote cards interspersed at even intervals.

Animations

Hero: staggered heroReveal entrance (same as Menu/Home). Masonry cards: fade-in on scroll via IntersectionObserver with staggered delays. Photos have subtle scale(1.03) hover. Lightbox: fade-in overlay + scale-up image.

Responsive

Desktop: 3 columns. Tablet (768px): 2 columns. Mobile (480px): 1 column. Hero adapts same as Menu page hero.

Transition to dark

Hero uses cream background (matching other pages). Bottom fade transitions from cream into the dark (#1E120B) masonry section — creating a dramatic shift into the gallery's moody atmosphere.
