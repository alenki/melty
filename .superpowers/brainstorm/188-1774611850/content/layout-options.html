A. Masonry Grid + Quotes

3-column masonry layout (like the Balance Coffee reference). Photos of varying heights flow naturally. Quote cards with warm cream backgrounds are interspersed. Subtle parallax scroll on images. Dark background.

B. Editorial / Magazine Layout
