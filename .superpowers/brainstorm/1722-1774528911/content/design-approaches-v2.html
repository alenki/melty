C. "Golden Arch" — Ornamental Frame (Recommended)

Warm cream-to-latte gradient with an elegant arch frame (like a cafe window or mirror) framing the form. Melty logo circle at the top, golden-accented star rating in a frosted pill, white-glass inputs with amber borders that warm on focus, and a chocolate-to-coffee gradient submit button. The arch creates a sense of ceremony — like writing in a guest book at an elegant establishment. Decorative but not busy.

Strengths

  • Distinctive architectural motif (the arch) — memorable
  • Perfect light-to-warm gradient progression
  • Ceremony without complexity — feels special
  • Award-winning potential through the arch framing device

Trade-offs

  • Arch needs precise sizing across breakpoints
