A. "Chocolate Warmth" — Vertical Flow

A single centered column on a deep chocolate gradient background with melting chocolate drip decoration at the top. Star rating with golden hover glow, frosted-glass textarea, optional contact field, and a warm amber submit button. The form feels like writing on parchment paper inside the cafe. Minimal, elegant, deeply warm.

Strengths

  • Most cohesive with existing site aesthetic
  • Focused — nothing competes for attention
  • Satisfying vertical rhythm

Trade-offs

  • Could feel simple if not carefully crafted
  • Less visual drama than alternatives

B. "Cafe Table" — Split Layout

A two-panel design inspired by sitting at a cafe table. Left side has decorative branding (logo, an inviting message in Russian, decorative dots). Right side is a pristine white card with the form — stars, textarea, contact, submit. The cream/latte background with the white card creates depth. Like opening a menu to write your thoughts.

Strengths

  • More visual interest and composition
  • Clear separation of brand storytelling and form
  • The white card "pops" against warm background

Trade-offs

  • Split layout may feel less immersive
  • More complex responsive behavior on mobile

C. "Candlelit Evening" — Dark Cinematic

Ultra-dark, moody atmosphere like the cafe at night. Deep chocolate-to-black gradient with floating golden particle lights (like candlelight reflections). Large cinematic star rating with amber glow effects. Form elements have subtle golden borders that glow on focus. The submit button pulses softly. Every interaction feels like touching warm light in darkness. Maximum atmosphere.

Strengths

  • Most visually dramatic and memorable
  • Matches the "dark and moody" brand aesthetic perfectly
  • Award-winning potential — truly distinctive

Trade-offs

  • Dark theme requires careful contrast for accessibility
  • May feel heavy if animations aren't well-tuned
