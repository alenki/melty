Design Details

Structure

  • Full viewport height (100vh / 100dvh)
  • Cream gradient background (same as home hero)
  • Organic SVG curves left & right with draw animation
  • 3 floating dots with gentle float animation
  • Subtle dot pattern background layer
  • Soft radial glow in center
  • Bottom fade gradient into content

Interactions

  • Pills hover: fill with chocolate bg, text goes cream
  • Click pill → smooth scroll to that category section
  • Staggered reveal: count → title → rule → subtitle → pills (wave)
  • Pills animate in with staggered 50ms delays
  • Scroll arrow bounces gently (2s infinite)
  • Sticky category bar activates after scrolling past hero
